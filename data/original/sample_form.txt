CONSENT FOR LAPAROSCOPIC CHOLECYSTECTOMY

I hereby authorize the surgical team to perform a laparoscopic cholecystectomy.
I understand that this procedure carries the following risks: postoperative
infection (2-5%), hemorrhage requiring transfusion (less than 1%), bile duct
injury (0.3-0.5%), and thromboembolism. Adverse cardiopulmonary events may
occur in patients with pre-existing cardiac conditions. In rare cases (about
2%), conversion to open surgery may be required.

Alternatives include non-surgical management with dietary modification and
observation, or open cholecystectomy. This procedure is contraindicated in
patients with uncorrected coagulopathy. The expected benefit is relief of
biliary colic and prevention of recurrent gallstone complications.
