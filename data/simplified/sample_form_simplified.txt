CONSENT FOR LAPAROSCOPIC CHOLECYSTECTOMY

I give my permission for the surgical team to do a laparoscopic cholecystectomy.

I understand that this surgery can cause the following problems: 

- Getting an infection after surgery (2-5%)
- Needing a blood transfusion because of bleeding (less than 1%)
- Damaging the bile duct (0.3-0.5%)
- Having a blood clot that can cause a heart problem (rare, about 2%)
- Having a heart problem that can cause breathing problems if you have a heart condition before surgery

Sometimes, the doctor might need to do the surgery in a different way (open surgery) if something goes wrong.

Other options are not doing the surgery at all (just eating healthy and watching what you eat) or doing the open surgery. This surgery is not good for people who have not fixed a blood problem.

The good thing about this surgery is that it can help you feel better if you have a stomach problem that hurts and can help you from getting sick again.
