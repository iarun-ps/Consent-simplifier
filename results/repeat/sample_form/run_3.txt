CONSENT FOR LAPAROSCOPIC (done through small cuts using a thin camera) CHOLECYSTECTOMY (surgery to remove the gallbladder)

I give permission for the surgical team to do a laparoscopic cholecystectomy.

I understand that this surgery can cause the following problems:
• Infection (2-5%)
• Need for blood transfusion (less than 1%)
• Injury to the bile duct (0.3-0.5%)
• Blood clots (thromboembolism (a blood clot that travels through the blood))
Adverse heart problems may happen to patients with pre-existing heart conditions.

In rare cases (about 2%), the surgery might need to be done the old way.

Other options are:
- Not doing surgery at all (non-surgical management)
- Making dietary changes and watching for signs
- Doing an open surgery

This surgery is not recommended for patients with uncorrected bleeding problems.

The expected benefit is relief from biliary colic (pain caused by gallstones) and preventing future gallstone problems.
